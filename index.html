Times accept 0700, 7:00, 7a or 7:30 PM. An end time at or before the start is read as an overnight shift, so 1900–0730 is 12.5 hours. The unpaid break is deducted from every day that has a shift and starts at 30 minutes on a new row, so 0700–1930 comes to 12.0 worked hours; change it per row, or set 0 for shifts paid straight through. Leave a day blank for a day off, or switch Hours from to Hours to type hours straight in.
wFTE = total scheduled worked hours per week ÷ (FTE hours ÷ weeks per year)  ·  pFTE = wFTE × benefit gross-up factor, so PTO coverage is included.
